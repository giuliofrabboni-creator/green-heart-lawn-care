What We Do

Complete Lawn Care Solutions

Every treatment is carefully timed and applied by a trained technician. No contractors, no shortcuts.

Fertiliser Treatments

Seasonal, professionally mixed fertiliser applications to feed your lawn exactly what it needs throughout the year.

Weed & Moss Control

Targeted weed and moss treatments that eliminate the problem without damaging your lawn's healthy grass.

Aeration

Core aeration relieves compaction, improves drainage and allows vital nutrients to penetrate deep into the root zone.

Scarification

Deep mechanical scarification removes thatch and dead material, reinvigorating tired, compacted lawns.

Overseeding

Professional overseeding fills bare patches with premium grass varieties suited to your soil type and sun exposure.

Lawn Renovation

Full lawn makeovers for severely neglected lawns, combining aeration, scarification, overseeding and soil improvement.
